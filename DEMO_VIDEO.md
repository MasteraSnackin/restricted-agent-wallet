The video explains the problem, authority split, allowed payment, cap and
allow-list rejection, owner revocation, post-revocation failure and the local
evidence boundary. It shows the behaviour of the recorded LocalNet prototype.

The video is not independent proof of a currently running ledger, DevNet
deployment, production authentication, an LLM integration or an MCP server.
The source, tests and dated evidence notes are the inspectable evidence for
those local claims.
